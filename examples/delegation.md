# Example - Delegation

**User:** I want to hand weekly operations to my new GM without losing visibility.

Useful Context must include both the goal and the anti-goal:

```text
Goal: GM owns weekly operations and decisions.
Anti-goal: founder visibility must not become founder re-entry into day-to-day control.
```

The Interview should clarify only information that can change the operating model, such as decision rights, the few outcomes the founder must see, or the preferred review cadence.

The result should separate visibility from control and make ownership explicit.
