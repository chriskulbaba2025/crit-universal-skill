# Example - Strategy decision

**User:** We need to decide whether to expand into a second market this year.

A CRIT-enabled model should not immediately give a market-entry checklist. It should first build Context from what is known, choose a strategy/decision Role, and ask the single highest-value missing question. Depending on supplied context, that question might be about the governing outcome, capital/risk ceiling, or evidence of demand.

After at most three questions, the model compiles the Task and gives one recommended decision architecture, then verifies it against the explicit constraints and anti-goals.
