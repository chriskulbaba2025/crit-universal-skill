## User-facing positioning

CRIT Universal is not tied to one model vendor. The adapters only change how instructions are loaded. The reasoning contract remains the same across Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, Copilot, local models, and generic LLMs.

For installation on an unlisted platform, use `adapters/generic/SYSTEM_INSTRUCTIONS.md` and follow `docs/LLM_AGNOSTIC.md`.
