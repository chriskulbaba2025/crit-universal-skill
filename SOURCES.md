# Sources and Attribution

## Primary public method source

The framework name and four-part sequence are derived from the public *The AI-Driven Leader* CRIT™ episode with Geoff Woods and Josh Wand, in which the framework is stated as **Context, Role, Interview, Task** and demonstrated live.

Spotify episode supplied during the source study:
- https://open.spotify.com/episode/0vKnjT8mqC4oW4Yybfbm6f

## Independent implementation

This repository's cross-platform adapters, routing logic, revision-preservation rules, verification layer, semantic quality gate, examples, validator, documentation, and branding are independently authored for this project.

The source conversation demonstrates behaviors that informed the implementation, including:

- Context that includes an anti-goal;
- identity/context separation when speaking for another person;
- a constrained Interview of one question at a time, up to three;
- iterative critique after the first draft;
- explicit preservation of previously good content during revision;
- top-down decomposition into subordinate layers;
- the need to verify hard constraints rather than assume model compliance.
